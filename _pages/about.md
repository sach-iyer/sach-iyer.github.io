## Hi! 

I'm Sachin, an early career professional interested in tackling the challenges Indians face through a policy and systems-change lens. This website serves to document my work and my thoughts.

## My work

I'm currently at [Dvara Health Finance (DHF)](https://dvarahealthfinance.com/), working to ensure spending on health translates to better financial and health outcomes for Indians.

I stay in [Vaduj](https://maps.app.goo.gl/pdJXsgswCECfk5aa9), a small town in [beautiful South Maharashtra](https://sach-iyer.github.io/satara-pictures/), where our field operations is currently based.

Through my work I get to travel across rural Maharashtra to understand both the challenges of rural healthcare access, and the intricacies of rural healthcare delivery.

With training in computational biology and an interest in policy and systems change, I am working at the forefront of healthcare delivery innovation in India. Check out [my LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sachin-g-iyer-a0ab38213/) for my work-related updates.
