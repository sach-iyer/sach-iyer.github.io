## Hi! 

Thanks for visiting my website. This is where I document my work and share my thoughts.

## My work

I'm currently at [Dvara Health Finance (DHF)](https://dvarahealthfinance.com/), working to ensure spending on health translates to better financial and health outcomes for Indians.

I stay in [Vaduj](https://maps.app.goo.gl/pdJXsgswCECfk5aa9), a small town in [beautiful South Maharashtra](https://sach-iyer.github.io/satara-pictures/), where our field operations is currently based.

Through my work I get to travel across rural Maharashtra and understand both the challenges of rural healthcare access, and the intricacies of rural healthcare delivery.

With training in computational biology and an interst in policy and systems change, I am working at the forefront of healthcare delivery innovations in India.
