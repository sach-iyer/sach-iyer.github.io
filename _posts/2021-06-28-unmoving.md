> On the 
---

<center>

So often romanticised                                 \
the great misalignment                                \
of what you want                                      \
and what you need.                                    \
But grossly unappreciated are the times,              \
when what you want is what you need-                  \
and it lies well within arm’s reach,                  \
but your useless arms with atrophied muscles          \
hang helplessly by your side.                         \
Unmoving, no matter how hard you will them to move.   \
And you will them.                                    \
And you will them.                                    \
And you will them.                                    \
And they hang helplessly by your side-                \
Unmoving.


</center>
