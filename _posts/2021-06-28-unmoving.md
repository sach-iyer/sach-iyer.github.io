> On the frustration of persistent failure

---

<div style="text-align: center;">

So often romanticised                                 <br>
the great misalignment                                <br>
of what you want                                      <br>
and what you need.                                    <br>
But grossly unappreciated are the times,              <br>
when what you want is what you need-                  <br>
and it lies well within arm’s reach,                  <br>
but your useless arms with atrophied muscles          <br>
hang helplessly by your side.                         <br>
Unmoving, no matter how hard you will them to move.   <br>
And you will them.                                    <br>
And you will them.                                    <br>
And you will them.                                    <br>
And they hang helplessly by your side-                <br>
Unmoving.                                             

</div>
